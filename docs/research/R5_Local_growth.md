# R5 Local growth: NUMA Wellness Collective: Instagram Growth Playbook for Mobile, AL (2025–2026 Ranking Rules)

> Deep-research result for prompt R5, received 2026-11-13. Orchestrator review notes: docs/research/REVIEW.md. WARNING: this report says no Numa context was supplied and assumes facts (classes, offers such as SHIFT/JAGS codes and a Krewe pass, partnerships with USA and hospitals). Offers, codes, partnerships and paid boosts are business decisions for the owners; the plugin may only use the tactics' content ideas and measurement rules, never invent offers.

NUMA's fastest credible path from under 5k followers is to publish 3–5 original, locally specific Reels and carousels per week. Each post should be built to earn *sends per reach* and *watch time*. New ideas get tested first as Trial Reels. Collab posts with Midtown neighbors and local creators are the main way to borrow audiences. Profile and Google Business Profile changes make the account easy to find. Every post must be NUMA's own footage, because since April 30, 2026 an account that mostly reposts loses recommendations to non-followers. The upside has limits. Organic Instagram engagement is falling: Quid/Rival IQ's 2026 all-industry median is 0.30%, and Health & Beauty is 0.14%. Even well-run local studios in agency case studies typically add about 1,000–2,000 followers over a quarter, not tens of thousands. Expect Instagram to be one channel among several, not the sole booking engine.

## TL;DR

- **What the algorithm rewards (official):** Adam Mosseri said in January 2025 that "the top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." Likes weigh slightly more with followers and sends slightly more with non-followers. Since April 30, 2026, accounts that mainly post content they didn't create or materially edit lose eligibility for recommendations, now for photos and carousels as well as Reels. NUMA must film everything itself and use Collab, Repost or materially edited formats, never plain re-uploads.
- **What the studies say works:** In Buffer's study of 2.1M posts, posting 3–5 times per week roughly doubled follower growth versus 1–2 times (0.26% vs 0.12% weekly). Metricool's 2026 study of 24.4M posts found Reels reach about 29% more people than carousels, while carousels earn about 9x the saves of single images. So use Reels to find people and carousels to convert and retain them.
- **What to run:** a 90-day test plan covering the 2027 Mobile Mardi Gras season (Jan 22–Feb 9, 2027). Days 1–30 fix discovery and set a baseline. Days 31–60 scale Trial Reels and Collab posts aimed at shift workers and University of South Alabama students. Days 61–90 run a Mardi Gras "recover between parades" series. Every phase has numeric pass/fail thresholds and a fallback.

## Assumptions about NUMA (stated explicitly)

No prior NUMA context was supplied, so this plan assumes:
- NUMA is a boutique wellness collective in or near Midtown Mobile.
- It offers traditional and/or infrared sauna, cold plunge (contrast sessions), and small-group movement classes (yoga, mobility, breathwork, possibly pilates).
- It sells intro offers, class packs and memberships.
- It has a public Instagram **professional (business) account** with roughly 1,000–4,000 followers, so it clears the 200-follower Trial Reels bar.
- It has a Google Business Profile.
- One staff member can give the account about 5–8 hours a week.

Adjust thresholds if the follower base is very different.

**Claims policy for all recommended content:** no health or medical claims. That rules out "detox," "boosts immunity," "reduces inflammation," "heals," "burns fat," "treats anxiety" and "improves sleep." Use experiential language instead: "how it feels," "your post-shift reset," "quiet hour," "heat then cold." Use factual logistics (hours, temperatures, session length) and safety and etiquette guidance ("check with your doctor if you're pregnant or have a heart condition"). This protects legal standing and ad eligibility. It also fits Instagram's direction toward trusted, original content.

**Timing note:** today is November 13, 2026, so a 90-day plan starting Monday, Nov 16 runs to about Feb 13, 2027.
- **Days 61–90** fall directly over Mobile's 2027 Carnival parade season. Visit Mobile lists celebrations from January 22 to February 9, 2027, with Fat Tuesday on Feb 9 and "over 40 parades."
- **Gulf Coast heat** content is a summer play, so in this window it becomes "bank and schedule" work, plus winter "cold-front sauna" content.

## Key Findings

### 1. What Instagram/Meta have officially said (highest-confidence evidence)

- **Ranking signals.** Adam Mosseri (January 22, 2025, as reported by Social Media Today): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends. So when looking at your insights, pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." He also said: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content."
  - **Common misquote to discard:** the widely repeated claim that sends carry "3–5x more weight than likes" is not on the record. Keywords Everywhere's tracker notes that Mosseri's actual wording is "slightly more important."
  - **Saves** are not among Mosseri's top three, though they remain a useful proxy for content worth coming back to.
  - **Comments:** a later Mosseri remark (October 2026, reported secondhand by ZoomSphere) that Instagram focuses "a lot more" on sends than comments points the same way. I could not verify it at the primary source.
- **Originality rule (verified against official and primary-adjacent sources).** On **April 30, 2026**, Instagram for Creators published "Rewarding original creators on Instagram." It extended the Reels originality protections introduced in 2024 to photos and carousels: "Accounts, mainly the content aggregators that primarily post unoriginal content in photos or carousel posts, in addition to reels, will no longer be shown in places where we recommend content." TechCrunch's same-day report:
  - It is "fine" to use third-party material such as meme templates or clips if you materially edit it by adding an element that enhances it.
  - The change "won't affect how Instagram shows people content from aggregator accounts they follow."
  - Instagram's live session described the recovery rule: an account becomes eligible again "when the majority [of] your content is original over 30 days." Account Status in Settings shows whether you are currently recommendable.
  - Instagram also cited "75% of recommendations in the US" now coming from original posts. That figure first appeared on Meta's Q4 2025 earnings call (January 28, 2026), where it was described as "up 10 percentage points" in the quarter, per secondary reports including MediaPost; the April 30 post repeated it.
- **Unverified or contradictory originality claims to ignore:**
  - Marketing blogs repeat that "10+ reposts in a rolling 30-day window" triggers exclusion and that original content gets "40–60% more distribution," both attributed to Tubefilter. Neither number appears in Instagram's own post or the TechCrunch coverage. Treat them as unverified.
  - The official rule is about the *majority* of recent posts, not a fixed count.
  - Blogs also claim that adding a border, watermark or caption doesn't count as material editing. That matches Instagram's live-session examples, where "no text, low value text or a spammy caption" did not qualify, but the exact border wording is secondhand.
- **Trial Reels.** Launched December 10, 2024 (Instagram for Creators). They are "shown to non-followers first" and can be shared to followers manually or automatically if they perform well.
  - **Eligibility:** the US-English Help Center, checked by several outlets in Sept–Oct 2026, requires a public account that is eligible for recommendations, with **200 followers for professional accounts** and 1,000 for personal ones. The UK-English Help Center reportedly words this differently, so this is an open contradiction.
  - **Scheduling:** Mosseri announced on April 2, 2026 (per Social Media Today) that Trial Reels can now be scheduled: "Back in November, someone asked why can't you schedule a Trial Reel. And so we built it."
  - **Instagram's own result (June 2025):** "40% of creators started posting reels more often and of those who did, 80% saw an increase in reels reach from non-followers." That is a self-selected group, not a controlled study.
  - **Re-posting penalty:** the Help Center warns a trial may get limited reach if the same content was already shared.
- **Hashtags.** Instagram announced on December 18, 2025 (per Social Media Today) that it would "gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to 5," down from 30. Mosseri: "a few specific tags actually perform better than a long list of generic ones." Earlier, he said hashtags "won't necessarily increase your content's visibility."
- **Collabs.** Per the Help Center (as cited by multiple guides in Oct 2026), you can add up to five collaborators. The post appears on each collaborator's profile and is shared with their followers.
- **Watermarks.** Per Social Media Today's report of a Mosseri myth-busting video, brand logos carry no penalty, but "Reels with TikTok and YouTube watermarks will cop a reach penalty"; the policy dates to 2021, when Instagram said watermarked reposted TikToks would be made "less discoverable" (Fast Company).
- **Google Business Profile (Google official).** You can add one social link per platform, including Instagram, via Edit profile → Contact → Social profiles. The feature is "available in select regions." Google "does not currently provide performance metrics or click tracking for social media links," and some profiles show links automatically.

### 2. Measured studies (correlational, large-sample; useful for setting expectations, not proof of cause)

| Study | Sample | Finding relevant to NUMA |

| Buffer (State of Social Engagement) | 52M+ posts | Reels about 36% more reach than carousels. Carousels about 12% more engagement. Replying to comments associated with +21% engagement. |
| Metricool 2026 Instagram Study | 24.4M posts, 375,118 accounts | Reels reach 29% more people than carousels and 212% more than single images. Carousels get about 20% more views than Reels and 9x the saves of single images. 70% of views arrive within 3 days. Reels shares +67% YoY. 21% of sub-10K accounts moved up a follower tier. |
| Socialinsider 2026 benchmarks | 35M–67.5M brand posts | Average engagement by followers 0.48% in 2025 (carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, images 0.37%). Q3 2026: 0.45% (carousels 0.50%, Reels 0.48%, images 0.35%). Reels reach about 9.8% of followers for accounts under 5K. Reels of 30–60s had the best reach rate (5.60%). |

**Where the studies conflict:** On reach, Socialinsider's reach study has carousels slightly ahead of Reels (4.50% vs 4.10%), while Buffer and Metricool have Reels ahead. On engagement rate, Buffer's reach-based figures (carousels 6.90%, Reels 3.31%) and the follower-based figures from Socialinsider and Rival IQ differ by an order of magnitude because the denominators differ. **Judge NUMA against its own baseline, not industry averages.**

## Details: 25 Growth Tactics for NUMA

Evidence key:
- **[O]** official Instagram, Meta or Google statement or doc
- **[M]** measured, large-sample study
- **[C]** case study (agency-published unless noted)
- **[P]** practitioner opinion or anecdote

Effort and impact are rated Low, Med or High. Unless noted, "Insights" refers to per-post Insights and the Professional Dashboard.

---

**T01 — Keyword the Name field, bio and handle for local search**
- **How for NUMA:** Change the Name field (separate from the @handle; about 30 characters) to something like "NUMA | Sauna & Cold Plunge Mobile." Bio line 1: "Midtown Mobile sauna, cold plunge + yoga." Line 2: the hours, including "early & late hours for shift workers." Line 3: the student rate. Add the address via the business contact button.
- **Effort:** Low. **Impact:** Med for search discovery, Low for Explore.
- **Evidence:** [O] Instagram guidance quoted by Buffer: "we look at that bio when we index results." [P] Broad consensus that the Name field is searchable (Buffer, SEO Sherpa). There is no official statement on how much it is weighted.
- **Measure:** profile visits; follows; whether NUMA appears when you search "sauna Mobile," "cold plunge Mobile" and "yoga Midtown Mobile" in Instagram (log rank weekly from a logged-out or secondary account).
- **If flat after 4 weeks:** test another keyword order ("Cold Plunge + Sauna | Mobile AL"). Instagram limits Name changes to two within 14 days (the in-app message, first spotted by Jane Manchun Wong in 2020), so change once and wait. Then move search effort into captions (T11).

**T02 — Link Google Business Profile ↔ Instagram, and tag the website link**
- **How for NUMA:** In GBP go to Edit profile → Contact → Social profiles → Instagram (https://www.instagram.com/{handle}). Put a UTM-tagged booking link in the Instagram bio (utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio). Add the Instagram icon to the website footer. Post the same weekly event to GBP posts and Stories. Ask GBP reviewers to tag NUMA when they post.
- **Effort:** Low. **Impact:** Low–Med. The value is cross-channel trust, not rankings.
- **Evidence:** [O] Google Help: one link per platform, "select regions," no click metrics. [P] There's no good evidence that social links move Map Pack positions. The Brazil Pilates case found 36% of sign-ups came from Google search, which argues for treating GBP as a core channel.
- **Measure:** Instagram "external link taps"; website analytics sessions with utm_source=instagram; GBP "website clicks" and "calls."
- **If flat:** the link exists for verification, so keep it. Shift effort to GBP posts and reviews and to the bio-link offer page (T19).

**T03 — Originality audit and Account Status check (protect eligibility)**
- **How for NUMA:**
  - Check Settings → Account Status today, and then monthly.
  - Stop re-uploading other accounts' clips, wellness quote graphics and trending memes without material changes.
  - Film everything in-house: steam on cedar, the plunge thermometer, instructor cues.
  - Share members' and partners' posts with the Repost button or as a Collab, never as a downloaded re-upload.
  - Export Reels from Edits or natively from Instagram with no TikTok or YouTube watermarks.
- **Effort:** Low. **Impact:** High for downside protection, because losing eligibility zeroes out non-follower reach.
- **Evidence:** [O] Instagram for Creators, Apr 30, 2026; TechCrunch, Apr 30, 2026; Instagram's live session (30-day majority-original recovery); Mosseri on watermark penalties (via Social Media Today). The "10+ reposts" threshold is unverified.
- **Measure:** Account Status shows "eligible for recommendations"; share of reach from non-followers (Insights → Views/Reach → Followers vs Non-followers).
- **If flat:** if non-follower share stays below about 30% on Reels after 4 weeks of 100% original posting, re-check Account Status and appeal any flags. Also check for recycled clips: Trial Reels get limited reach if Instagram detects previously shared content.

**T04 — Commit to 3–5 feed posts per week (Reels-heavy), with zero skipped weeks**
- **How for NUMA:** Base week: 3 Reels and 1 carousel (plus Stories daily, T17). Batch-film 2 hours a week in the studio. Stretch goal in the Mardi Gras weeks: 6–7 posts.
- **Effort:** Med. **Impact:** High.
- **Evidence:** [M] Buffer: 0.26% weekly growth at 3–5 posts vs 0.12% at 1–2; 0.44% at 6–9; zero-post weeks fall below baseline. The study is correlational and compares each account with itself. [C] The Manchester yoga studio grew 400 → 2,400 on 3 posts/week; the London PT studio grew +91% on daily posting.
- **Measure:** weekly net follows (Professional Dashboard → Followers), total weekly reach, reach per post.
- **If flat:** if weekly follower growth stays below 0.25% after 4 weeks at 3–5 posts, the problem is content, not volume. Go to T05, T06 and T08 before adding more posts.

**T05 — Engineer the first 2 seconds and the length for watch time**
- **How for NUMA:**
  - Open on motion or a "pattern interrupt": ice cracking as a hand breaks the plunge surface; the sauna door opening onto steam; a sweating nurse in scrubs at 7:10am saying "post-night-shift reset."
  - Put the payoff text on screen in frame 1 ("What 3 rounds of heat→cold looks like at NUMA").
  - Aim for 20–45 seconds.
  - Loop the ending back into the opening shot.
- **Effort:** Med. **Impact:** High, because watch time is a top-3 signal.
- **Evidence:** [O] Mosseri lists watch time as a top-3 signal and tells creators to track average watch time. [M] Socialinsider: 30–60s Reels had the best reach rate (5.60%); Reels over 2 minutes 3.50%. [C] Instagram's creator spotlight on @erekosima43 credits a "hook… loop… series title" structure.
- **Measure:** Reel Insights → average watch time; skip rate where shown; views; non-follower reach.
- **If flat:** if average watch time stays under about 40% of Reel length, cut Reels to 12–20s with one idea each. Test 3 alternative hooks for the same footage as Trial Reels (T08).

**T06 — Make "send-able" content on purpose (sends per reach)**
- **How for NUMA:** Build posts people forward to a specific friend:
  - "Tag the coworker you're dragging to sauna after your 3rd night shift"
  - "Send this to your parade crew: our Joe Cain Day recovery hours"
  - "Your USA study group's Sunday reset"
  - "Midtown date idea that isn't dinner"
  - "POV: you said you'd try the cold plunge"

  End with a soft "send this to the friend who…" rather than "like and follow."
- **Effort:** Low–Med. **Impact:** High for non-follower reach.
- **Evidence:** [O] Mosseri: sends are "slightly more important for unconnected content"; track sends per reach. [M] Metricool: Reels shares up 67% YoY.
- **Measure:** shares ÷ reach per post. This is the closest proxy, because Insights reports "Shares" rather than DM sends separately. Track it in a sheet.
- **If flat:** if sends per reach stays under about 0.5% on Reels, rewrite around a named person or occasion ("your charge nurse," "your krewe"). Test the person-specific CTA against no CTA as paired Trial Reels.

**T07 — Saveable carousels: guides, schedules, etiquette**
- **How for NUMA:** Monthly carousels:
  - "Your first contrast session at NUMA: what to bring, how long, etiquette"
  - "NUMA hours mapped to 7a–7p / 7p–7a shifts"
  - "Mardi Gras weekend: open hours by parade day"
  - "Sauna etiquette 101"
  - "Who should check with a doctor first"

  Use 6–10 slides with a keyword-rich title slide. No health-benefit claims.
- **Effort:** Med. **Impact:** Med for reach, High for conversion and retention.
- **Evidence:** [M] Metricool: carousels get 9x the saves of single images and about 20% more views than Reels. Socialinsider: carousels have the top engagement rate (0.50–0.55%). Buffer: carousels +12% engagement. [P] Instagram re-shows carousels to people who didn't swipe (Metricool's interpretation).
- **Measure:** saves ÷ reach; shares; profile visits and follows from the post.
- **If flat:** if saves per reach stays under about 1%, the content isn't useful enough. Make it more concrete (times, prices, what to wear) and test a shorter 4-slide version.

**T08 — Run a Trial Reels testing program (2–3 per week)**
- **How for NUMA:**
  - Confirm eligibility: public professional account, 200+ followers, eligible for recommendations.
  - Each week, post 2–3 *new* Reels as Trials: hook variants, a student-angle version, a shift-worker version.
  - Schedule them for local peak hours.
  - Promote winners to followers manually after 24–72h. Archive losers. Never re-upload the same file as a normal Reel.
- **Effort:** Med. **Impact:** Med–High for learning speed and non-follower reach.
- **Evidence:** [O] Instagram for Creators (Dec 2024); Help Center (200/1,000 followers; limited reach for previously shared content); Instagram, June 2025: 40% posted more often, and 80% of those saw higher non-follower reach (self-reported). [P] A 14-day single-account test (CreatorHouse) found trials got about 2.3x non-follower reach but about 0.6x engagement. Treat Trial Reels as a testing tool, not the main posting method.
- **Measure:** Trial insights at 24h: views, likes, comments, shares. Compare against NUMA's previous trials. Track the win rate (share of trials beating NUMA's median Reel views).
- **If flat:** if no trial beats the median after 8 trials, the topics, not the hooks, are wrong. Switch angles (heat/cold → people/community → behind-the-scenes). If the toggle is missing, check follower count and Account Status.

**T09 — Collab posts with Midtown and Mobile neighbors (borrow audiences)**
- **How for NUMA:** Co-author Reels (up to 5 collaborators) with:
  - a Midtown coffee shop ("sauna then latte" route)
  - a local run club ("Saturday run → plunge")
  - a juice or smoothie spot
  - a physical therapy or massage practice (no clinical claims)
  - local photographers

  Film the content yourself so the post is original. Pitch each partner a ready-to-post draft.
- **Effort:** Med. **Impact:** High.
- **Evidence:** [O] Help Center: collaborators' followers are shown the post; up to 5 collaborators. The April 2026 originality guidance points creators to Collabs instead of re-uploading. [C] The Pearl NYC × Truvani event collab (SmartHealthClubs) is anecdotal with no numbers. No measured study isolates the collab lift.
- **Measure:** Insights for the collab post: reach split by followers vs non-followers, follows from the post, profile visits. Compare with NUMA's median Reel.
- **If flat:** if a collab's non-follower reach isn't at least 1.5x NUMA's median Reel, the partner audience isn't local or engaged. Prioritise partners with high local engagement over high follower counts.

**T10 — Local micro-creator and student creator partnerships (as Collabs)**
- **How for NUMA:** Recruit 3–5 Mobile-area micro-creators (1k–20k followers): a nurse-lifestyle creator, a University of South Alabama student creator, a Mobile food or events account, a local fitness coach. Offer free sessions in exchange for one Collab Reel that they film (their footage counts as original). Brief the no-claims rule in writing. Use Instagram's paid-partnership label when compensated.
- **Effort:** Med. **Impact:** Med–High.
- **Evidence:** [O] Originality guidance: creator content shared via Collab or made for the brand counts as original; reposted creator content does not (per Disrupt Marketing's summary of the update). [C] Chilli Pilates used 100k+ influencer outreach, with Instagram followers +10% (no baseline given). [P] Most local-influencer advice is anecdotal.
- **Measure:** collab post follows, profile visits, non-follower reach; promo-code redemptions ("NURSE," "JAGS") at booking.
- **If flat:** if a creator collab drives fewer than 10 follows and 0 code redemptions, drop that creator tier. Move budget to member-ambassadors (T16).

**T11 — Keyword captions, five or fewer hashtags, and location tags**
- **How for NUMA:** Open each caption with a plain-language phrase people search: "Sauna and cold plunge in Midtown Mobile." Use 3–5 specific hashtags (#MobileAL, #MidtownMobile, #ColdPlunge, #SaunaCulture, #MobileMardiGras). Always add a location tag (NUMA's address or Midtown). Write alt text on carousels.
- **Effort:** Low. **Impact:** Low–Med.
- **Evidence:** [O] 5-hashtag cap (announced Dec 18, 2025, down from 30); Mosseri says hashtags don't necessarily increase visibility. [P] Hootsuite's 2022 experiment found keyword captions out-reached hashtag-heavy posts. Claims of a "30% more reach in 2026" Hootsuite test are unverified. [C] The Manchester yoga studio credits neighbourhood tags with showing up in local search within about 3 weeks.
- **Measure:** Insights → reach by source where shown (Search/Explore vs Home vs Profile); profile visits from the post.
- **If flat:** keep the hygiene (it's nearly free). Don't expect reach from it, and reallocate effort to T05 and T06.

**T12 — Mardi Gras "Recover Between Parades" content series**

  - Reels: "post-parade-night contrast session," "krewe recovery Sunday," "MoonPie count vs. plunge rounds" (lighthearted).

- **Effort:** Med–High. **Impact:** High for seasonal local relevance and sends.
- **Evidence:** [O] Visit Mobile 2027 dates. [C] The Manchester case found neighbourhood and seasonal posts reached more local accounts. [P] Seasonal and local-event content is a standard practitioner tactic. Reported University of South Alabama Mardi Gras closures (Feb 8–9, 2027) come from a secondary calendar site; verify with USA.
- **Measure:** sends per reach, non-follower reach (local), "Krewe pass" redemptions, Story link taps on the hours carousel.
- **If flat:** if Mardi Gras posts don't beat NUMA's median Reel by 25% in sends or reach, cut the volume. Keep only the hours carousel and the best-performing angle, and Collab more with parade-adjacent businesses.

**T14 — Healthcare shift-worker program plus content pillar**
- **How for NUMA:**
  - Offer early and late slots (e.g., 6–8am and 8–10pm) and a "Shift Reset" pass with healthcare ID verification.
  - Content: "Your 7:30am after-shift routine," "Night-shift Tuesday quiet hour," nurse-hosted Q&As about what they like about the space.

- **Effort:** Med. **Impact:** Med–High. This is a dense, underserved, word-of-mouth-heavy audience.
- **Evidence:** [P] Audience-specific offers combined with Collab distribution is practitioner logic. [C] The London PT studio found a single-offer booking page more than doubled enquiries from link clicks.
- **Measure:** sends per reach on shift-worker Reels; "SHIFT" code redemptions; Story link taps; DM inquiries mentioning shifts.
- **If flat:** if fewer than 5 Shift Reset passes are sold in 4 weeks, test the offer (price, hours) before the content. Move to in-person flyers in hospital break rooms with a QR code to the Instagram profile.

**T15 — University of South Alabama student program**
- **How for NUMA:** A student rate with a .edu or ID check; "finals week reset" and "Sunday study-group reset" Reels; 3–4 campus ambassadors filming their own Collab Reels; tie-ins with semester start (January) and finals. Don't use USA logos or marks without permission.

**T16 — Member stories without breaking originality (Collab, Repost button or materially edited)**
- **How for NUMA:** Film members with written consent: "first plunge reaction," "why I come at 6am." When members post about NUMA, use the native Repost or reshare it to Stories, or invite them as a Collab. Never download and re-upload. Testimonials must describe experience, not health outcomes.
- **Effort:** Low–Med. **Impact:** Med.

- **Measure:** likes per reach (follower resonance), saves, DM inquiries after testimonial posts.
- **If flat:** if member content underperforms the median by more than 30%, switch from talking heads to unscripted reaction moments.

**T17 — Daily Stories that convert: polls, booking link, countdowns**
- **How for NUMA:** 3–6 frames a day:
  - "Plunge temp today: 48°F"
  - an open-slots poll
  - a link sticker to the booking page
  - a countdown sticker for Mardi Gras hours
  - Q&A boxes ("first-timer questions")

**T18 — First-hour community management (reply to every comment and DM fast)**
- **How for NUMA:** For 60 minutes after each post, reply to every comment with a question back, and answer DMs within 10 minutes during staffed hours. Before posting, spend 10 minutes engaging with local accounts (Midtown businesses, krewes, USA organizations).
- **Effort:** Med (ongoing). **Impact:** Med.

**T19 — Comment-keyword → DM booking path, plus a single-offer landing page**

- **Effort:** Med. **Impact:** Med for bookings; Low–Med for reach (comments aren't a top-3 signal).

**T21 — Post when NUMA's followers and Mobile locals are active**
- **How for NUMA:** Use Insights → Total followers → Most active times. Expect bimodal shift-worker windows (about 7–9am and 8–10pm Central) as well as typical evenings. Schedule Trial Reels for non-follower peaks.
- **Effort:** Low. **Impact:** Low.

**T24 — Attribution field: "How did you hear about us?" at every booking**
- **How for NUMA:** Add a required source field to the booking flow (Instagram / Google / Friend / Event / Hospital / USA / Other). Add a follow-up field for "Which post?"

**Day-90 overall success definition:**
- Followers up 35–75% from baseline. For context, the best agency cases show +91% to +500% from small bases, and the median brand grows far less.

If followers grow but bookings don't, the problem is the offer and the DM path, not the algorithm.

## Caveats

- **Official vs folklore:** Only Mosseri's three signals, the April 30, 2026 originality rules, Trial Reels mechanics and eligibility, the 5-hashtag cap and the collaborator limit are confirmed by Instagram. "Sends = 3–5x likes," "10 reposts in 30 days," "original gets 40–60% more distribution," "+30% reach from keywords in 2026" and "+73% retention from daily Stories" are unverified. They appear only in secondary blogs.
- **Help Center inconsistencies:** The US Help Center lists 200 followers for professional accounts to use Trial Reels. Earlier reporting (July 2025) said 1,000, and the UK Help Center reportedly words it differently. If the toggle is missing, check Account Status.
- **Benchmarks conflict by method:** follower-based engagement (0.30–0.48%) and reach-based engagement (3–7%) can't be compared. Socialinsider's reach data puts carousels slightly above Reels, while Buffer and Metricool put Reels ahead.
- **Case studies are weak evidence:** Every studio case found was published by the agency that ran it. Two don't name the studio. Polestar's is undated and possibly pre-2023. The only sauna/cold-plunge case (Therma Haus) was paid. None are from Alabama or the Gulf Coast. No independent, organic sauna or cold-plunge growth case with numbers was found.
- **Local facts to verify:** University of South Alabama Mardi Gras closure dates (Feb 8–9, 2027) come from a secondary calendar site. Hospital partnership interest is assumed, not confirmed.
- **Wellness claims:** this report did not research FTC or Meta advertising policy in detail. Get local legal review before running paid ads.
