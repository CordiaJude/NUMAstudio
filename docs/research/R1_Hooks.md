# R1 Hooks: Scroll-Stopping Hooks for NUMA Wellness Collective: A 32-Pattern Catalogue for Reels, Carousels, Photos and Stories (2025–2026)

> Deep-research result for prompt R1 (docs/RESEARCH_PROMPTS_PLAYBOOK.md), received 2026-11-13. Imported into the playbook by `strategy.py import-research`. Orchestrator review notes are in docs/research/REVIEW.md.

For a small contrast-therapy studio like NUMA, a scroll gets stopped by something physical and real happening on screen at frame 0: a body entering 39°F water, steam bursting off sauna stones, a face reacting, or a crowd at Numa Nights. Overlay that with a 4–7 word line that tells a stranger in Mobile what they're looking at. Clever copy over a static room shot does not do this job. No published study isolates hook types for wellness accounts. So the hard evidence covers mechanics (skip rate, sends, the carousel second chance, Story frame-1 exits) and format performance. Everything at the pattern level is either observed (named accounts, with numbers) or practitioner opinion, and each pattern below is labelled accordingly.

## TL;DR

- **The measured mechanics favor a sensory, readable frame 0.** Instagram scores the hook directly: skip rate is the share of views that skipped in the first 3 seconds, and it replaced view rate in Reels Insights in August 2025. Adam Mosseri named watch time, likes and sends as the top three ranking signals, with sends "slightly more important for unconnected content." Reels that are understandable on mute and worth DMing to a friend are what reach non-followers in Mobile.
- **Use Reels to reach people and carousels to win saves.** For small accounts, Reels lead on reach: Metricool/HypeAuditor found a median of 134 views for Reels vs 56 for carousels and 23 for images at under 1,000 followers. Carousels win saves and engagement per person reached: Buffer's median engagement by reach is 6.9% for carousels vs 3.3% for Reels. Instagram re-serves unswiped carousels starting on slide 2, so every NUMA carousel needs two cover slides.
- **Test these 10 first, as Trial Reels or A/B pairs:** plunge-entry moment, steam-pour sensory, first-timer reaction face, on-screen timer challenge, local callout, $49 price-anchor, Numa Nights crowd energy, "send this to" duo, first-timer FAQ carousel with a rehook slide 2, and breathe-along. Every example line sells the experience (temperature, feeling, people, price, place) and never an outcome. That keeps you inside the FTC's rule that health claims need "competent and reliable scientific evidence."

**Conflicts to note.** Socialinsider measures a slightly *higher* reach rate for carousels (4.50%) than Reels (4.10%) across January 2025–August 2026, while Buffer and Metricool find Reels ahead on reach. The difference comes from method (reach divided by followers, vs raw reach) and sample (all brand sizes vs small accounts). For an account under 5,000 followers, the small-account data (Metricool/HypeAuditor) is the most relevant, and it favors Reels for discovery. Skip-rate "benchmarks" that circulate online (for example "under 20% is strong") have no primary source. Treat your own trailing median as the benchmark.

### What is observed (named accounts)

- **Remedy Place (LA/NYC/Boston):** The #6minuteclub ice-bath format is the clearest proven wellness hook. Business of Fashion wrote that the "#6minuteclub flex on Remedy Place's TikTok" includes videos with "over 10 million views," calling it "a very shareable moment on social media," but it named no specific video. TikTok's own discover page lists Remedy Place videos at 2.8M ("First timer"), 2.7M ("False start – tub 1 & 2"), 1.5M and 516.9K views. The account has about 171.4K TikTok followers and 3.7M total likes. Robb Report describes the feeds as "videos of people plunging into icy water, the shock on their faces as entertaining as the spunky music." Founder Dr. Jonathan Leary told Glossy, "We average [about] 1,000 ice baths a month," a figure covering all ice baths rather than just the roughly $50 class. *Caveat: these numbers are TikTok, not Instagram, and they come from a celebrity-adjacent brand.*
- **Othership (Toronto/NYC):** about 81K Instagram followers (up from 35.8K in mid-2023). On TikTok, its towel-waving explainer ("What is towel waving?", with on-screen text explaining the essential-oil pour and towel movement) drew 1,002 likes, against 49–122 for other brand posts. That's roughly 10× the account's norm, from a sensory, curiosity-driven explainer. CEO Robbie Bent says phones are kept out of the space on purpose: "You can't fully 'get' the Othership experience by looking at a picture or video, so it sparks curiosity… When you can't post, you talk about it."
- **Bathhouse (NYC):** about 287K Instagram followers (an earlier snapshot showed 186K). Its own TikToks are modest: "Bathhouse vs spa, what's the difference?" got 85 likes, and an amenity-upgrade post got 304. One creator video about the Williamsburg location drew about 35.5K likes. The pattern: guests and creators carry first-person reaction hooks better than the brand does.
- **SweatHouz/SWTHZ:** the company's own 2026 NYC press release claims "100+ studios nationwide and 1.9 million private recovery sessions delivered last year alone," yet visible social volume is mostly creator UGC "first cold plunge" reactions ("I only lasted 30 seconds in the cold plunge") with roughly 42–157 likes each. Local franchise accounts are small (for example @sweathouzhorizonwest, about 1,125 followers). SweatHouz's X account has posted claims like "reduce muscle soreness by up to 40%." That is exactly the kind of quantified health claim NUMA must avoid.
- **Small local studios:** comparable accounts sit in NUMA's range (Drip Sauna & Plunge about 3,369 followers, Sisu Sauna Studio about 4,224, Surge Sauna & Cold Plunge about 556). No verified breakout Reel from a sub-10K sauna studio turned up in this research.

### FTC guardrail (applies to every pattern)

The FTC's December 2022 Health Products Compliance Guidance says health-related claims require "competent and reliable scientific evidence," generally randomized controlled human trials. Testimonials and expert endorsements are treated as if the advertiser made the claim directly. So the hook and the payoff must sell **sensation, environment, people, ritual, price, schedule and place**. Avoid "detox," "reduces inflammation," "boosts immunity," "burns calories," "better sleep," "heals," and any percentage. Members can't say these for you either. Expert content at Clinical Conversations should be framed as questions and conversation, not promised results.

## Details: The Catalogue (32 Patterns)

**Legend for expected effects** (relative to NUMA's own median Reel or post; directional expectations, not measured per-pattern results): ↓↓ strong decrease, ↓ decrease, → neutral, ↑ increase, ↑↑ strong increase. For skip rate, ↓ is good.

**Evidence labels:** *measured* = large-sample study or Instagram/Meta statement backing the mechanism; *observed* = documented example from a named account; *opinion* = practitioner advice without primary data. Most patterns are observed or opinion at the pattern level, sitting on measured mechanics.

### 1. id: `plunge-entry` · name: The Plunge Moment
- **Formats:** reel, story
- **Template:** "[Person] / [water temp] / [round #]" with the body already entering the water at 0.0s
- **NUMA examples:** "39°F. Round two. No backing out." · "Her first plunge at NUMA. Watch her face."
- **Visual frame 0:** Feet or legs breaking the water surface of a plunge, mid-motion. Temperature text in the top third.
- **Best use:** community, local/seasonal, offer (end card)
- **Evidence:** observed. Remedy Place plunge videos at 2.7M–2.8M TikTok views, and Business of Fashion says some #6minuteclub videos have "over 10 million views." Robb Report describes "shock on their faces" as the draw. Measured mechanism: skip rate covers the first 3 seconds (Instagram, August 2025).
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends ↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** two posts in a row (fatigue), or with members who haven't consented to being filmed (sauna and plunge areas need explicit release).
- **If it underperforms:** move the cut earlier (start at water contact, not the step) or switch to `reaction-face`.

### 2. id: `reaction-face` · name: First-Timer Reaction
- **Formats:** reel, story
- **Template:** "[Name]'s first [plunge/round/breathwork] — [timer]"
- **NUMA examples:** "Marcus said he'd last 10 seconds. Watch." · "First plunge ever. Brave or unhinged?"
- **Visual frame 0:** Tight face shot at the instant of cold contact, eyes wide, with the plunge visible.
- **Best use:** community, offer
- **Evidence:** observed. Remedy Place's "First timer" at 2.8M TikTok views. SweatHouz creator UGC is built on "I only lasted 30 seconds" reactions. A Bathhouse creator review reached about 35.5K likes.
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends ↑↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** if the reaction looks like distress (it reads as unsafe), or with anyone who hasn't signed a media release.
- **If it underperforms:** pair two friends (`bring-a-friend-duo`) so the reaction becomes social, not solo.

### 3. id: `timer-challenge` · name: On-Screen Clock
- **Formats:** reel, story
- **Template:** "[Timer 00:00] — can [name] make it to [time]?"
- **NUMA examples:** "Goal: 3 minutes. Clock's running." · "Day 1: 0:45. Day 30 of Discover NUMA: ?"
- **Visual frame 0:** Large running timer graphic over a person in the plunge or sauna.
- **Best use:** community, offer (30-day $49 tie-in)
- **Evidence:** observed. Remedy Place's #6minuteclub turns time-in-water into a shareable achievement; Business of Fashion quotes it as "a very shareable moment." Measured mechanism: watch time is a top-3 signal (Mosseri, January 2025).
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time ↑↑ (people wait for the end) · sends ↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** never frame longer cold exposure as healthier or as a goal everyone should push toward. Keep it playful and include "go at your pace."
- **If it underperforms:** shorten the clock (30 to 60 seconds) and put the result in the first frame (`end-result-first`).

### 4. id: `steam-pour` · name: Löyly / Steam Burst
- **Formats:** reel, carousel (cover), story
- **Template:** "This is what [ritual name] sounds like" over water hitting stones
- **NUMA examples:** "The sound of a 14-person Finnish sauna waking up." · "Ladle. Stones. Löyly. Wait for the wave."
- **Visual frame 0:** Ladle mid-pour, steam already rising off the stones. Warm, dark, high contrast.
- **Best use:** education, local/seasonal
- **Evidence:** observed. Othership's towel-waving explainer got about 10× its normal likes (1,002 vs 49–122). Opinion: practitioner guides recommend opening on a sharp sound in the first half second.
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends → · saves ↑
- **When NOT to use:** if the footage is dim or noisy. Phone sensors struggle in sauna light, and a dark frame 0 raises skips.
- **If it underperforms:** add a 4-word text overlay ("What is löyly?") and a narrator line, turning it into `myth-or-question`.

### 5. id: `sound-first-asmr` · name: Sound Before Sight
- **Formats:** reel
- **Template:** Sharp natural sound at 0.0s (hiss, splash, door latch) plus "[Sound] = [moment]"
- **NUMA examples:** "That hiss = round one." · "The splash you'll hear every Thursday night."
- **Visual frame 0:** A close-up of the sound source (stones, water surface, cedar door) with the caption in large type for mute viewers.
- **Best use:** community, local/seasonal
- **Evidence:** opinion (practitioner guides, e.g. PromptsLove's scan of 79 outlier Reels). Caution: the oft-cited muted-viewing figures trace to a May 2016 Digiday report on Facebook, not Instagram, where publishers self-reported about 85% soundless views (PopSugar: 50–80%), so the text overlay is mandatory.
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ (with sound) / → (muted) · avg watch time ↑ · sends → · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** as the only hook. Muted viewers get nothing without text.
- **If it underperforms:** use `steam-pour` with stronger text, or swap to trending audio (`trend-audio-sync`).

### 6. id: `pov-first-visit` · name: POV Walk-In
- **Formats:** reel, story
- **Template:** "POV: your first [time/night] at [place]"
- **NUMA examples:** "POV: you finally booked the $49 first month." · "POV: Tuesday 6pm in Midtown Mobile."
- **Visual frame 0:** First-person shot already moving through the door or toward the sauna. Movement starts at frame 0, not after.
- **Best use:** offer, local/seasonal
- **Evidence:** opinion (widespread creator format). Observed: SweatHouz creator UGC leans on "first visit" walk-throughs.
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends ↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** if the walk-in shows an empty, unlit room. Emptiness reads as "nobody goes here."
- **If it underperforms:** start the POV at the most dramatic point (the plunge) and loop back to the door.

### 7. id: `temp-split` · name: Hot | Cold Split Screen
- **Formats:** reel, carousel, photo
- **Template:** "[Hot temp]° | [Cold temp]° — [short invite]"
- **NUMA examples:** "190° | 39°. Pick your order." · "From cedar heat to cold water in 10 steps."
- **Visual frame 0:** Vertical split: glowing sauna left, blue plunge right, two huge numbers.
- **Best use:** education, offer
- **Evidence:** opinion (practitioners list "split screen before/after" as a no-words visual hook). Uses measured facts NUMA controls (its own temperatures), which keeps it FTC-safe.
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time → · sends → · saves ↑ (carousel)
- **When NOT to use:** if temperatures vary day to day. Only post numbers you actually maintain.
- **If it underperforms:** add a human in each half, since faces outperform rooms in the observed examples.

### 8. id: `myth-or-question` · name: The Question First-Timers Actually Ask
- **Formats:** reel, carousel
- **Template:** "[Common question]?" + instructor answering on camera or slides
- **NUMA examples:** "Sauna first or plunge first? Here's how we run it at NUMA." · "Do I need to bring anything? (Short answer: a towel's on us.)"
- **Visual frame 0:** Instructor mid-sentence, looking at the lens, question in bold top-third text.
- **Best use:** education
- **Evidence:** opinion (practitioners: put the promise first). Measured: Metricool found posts with CTAs get 203% more comments, so end with "drop your question."
- **Expected effect:** skip → / ↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends → · saves ↑↑
- **When NOT to use:** if the honest answer requires a health claim ("will this help my arthritis?"). Redirect those to a physician, not a Reel.
- **If it underperforms:** convert it into a carousel (`first-timer-faq`), where saves are measured higher.

### 9. id: `first-timer-faq` · name: First-Timer Guide Carousel
- **Formats:** carousel
- **Template:** Cover: "Your first [visit]: [n] things to know" · Slide 2: standalone rehook
- **NUMA examples:** "First time at NUMA? 7 things nobody tells you." · "What to bring, wear and expect (in 60 seconds of swiping)."
- **Visual frame 0 (slide 1):** Real photo of the sauna with people, a large number, a warm palette. Slide 2: a different strong image plus "Swimsuit or towel? Both work."
- **Best use:** education, offer
- **Evidence:** measured. Carousels lead save rate (0.05% vs 0.04% Reels, Socialinsider). Buffer: 6.9% engagement by reach. The slide-2 re-serve is from Mosseri.
- **Expected effect:** skip n/a · swipe-through ↑ · sends → · saves ↑↑
- **When NOT to use:** as the only format in a week. Carousels deepen; they don't discover at your size.
- **If it underperforms:** add music (Mosseri: makes carousels eligible for Reels surfaces) and rework slide 2.

### 10. id: `expectation-vs-reality` · name: What It Actually Looks Like
- **Formats:** reel, carousel
- **Template:** "What you think [X] is / what it actually is at [place]"
- **NUMA examples:** "What you think a sauna studio is: silent, intimidating. What NUMA is: this." · "Breathwork: you expect weird. You get Tuesday-night calm with 12 neighbors."
- **Visual frame 0:** Split or quick-cut: stock-style "intimidating spa" vs real warm crowd.
- **Best use:** community, offer
- **Evidence:** observed. Bathhouse's own "Bathhouse vs spa, what's the difference?" overlay hook. Othership positions itself as social, not silent. Low numbers on brand posts (85 likes) suggest it works better voiced by people than by the brand.
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends ↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** if "reality" implies outcomes ("you'll leave pain-free").
- **If it underperforms:** let a member deliver it (`member-experience-voice`).

### 11. id: `price-anchor` · name: The $49 Line
- **Formats:** reel, photo, story, carousel
- **Template:** "$[price]. [Time]. [What's included]."
- **NUMA examples:** "$49. Your first month. Sauna, plunge, red light, breathwork, yoga." · "Less than a night out downtown: one month of NUMA for $49."
- **Visual frame 0:** Price in huge type over motion (plunge or steam), never over a static logo.
- **Best use:** offer
- **Evidence:** opinion (direct-response practice). Measured context: Metricool found CTA posts get 92% more saves. FTC: state offer terms clearly (new clients, one month).
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ (for local intenders) / ↑ (for strangers) · avg watch time → · sends ↑ · saves ↑
- **When NOT to use:** in Trial Reels to cold non-local audiences. The price means nothing outside Mobile.
- **If it underperforms:** lead with `plunge-entry` and move the price to second 3 or the end card.

### 12. id: `local-callout` · name: Mobile, This Is Yours
- **Formats:** reel, photo, story
- **Template:** "[City/neighborhood], you have a [unique thing] now."
- **NUMA examples:** "Mobile, you have a 14-person Finnish sauna. In Midtown." · "Midtown Mobile's new Thursday night plan."
- **Visual frame 0:** Recognizable local cue (street sign, live oak, skyline) cutting straight into the sauna. Text names the city.
- **Best use:** local/seasonal
- **Evidence:** opinion. Measured mechanism: sends weigh more for non-follower reach (Mosseri). "Mobile" in text and caption helps location relevance (Mosseri has said hashtags help Instagram understand topic, not raise reach).
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ (local) · avg watch time → · sends ↑↑ (tagging friends) · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** in back-to-back weeks, because it reads as a billboard.
- **If it underperforms:** make it about a specific local moment (`seasonal-weather`).

### 13. id: `seasonal-weather` · name: Weather-Jacked Hook
- **Formats:** reel, story, photo
- **Template:** "It's [weather] in [city]. Here's [contrast]."
- **NUMA examples:** "92° and humid in Mobile. Our plunge is 39°." · "First cold front of the year. Perfect sauna night."
- **Visual frame 0:** Weather app screenshot or sweaty-street shot, hard cut to plunge or sauna.
- **Best use:** local/seasonal
- **Evidence:** opinion (timeliness).
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time → · sends ↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** after the moment passes. Post the same day.
- **If it underperforms:** turn it into a Story poll (`poll-first-frame`): "Hot or cold person today?"

### 14. id: `event-crowd` · name: Numa Nights Energy
- **Formats:** reel, carousel, story
- **Template:** "This was [event] last [day]. Next one: [date]."
- **NUMA examples:** "Numa Nights, last Friday. Next one's on the calendar." · "14 people, one sauna, one playlist."
- **Visual frame 0:** Wide shot of a full sauna or lounge, people laughing, already mid-action.
- **Best use:** event, community
- **Evidence:** observed. Othership's "night out" positioning ("the new 'night out'"). Remedy Place's "Cooling Down Sunset" group plunge events.
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends ↑↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** if the event was sparsely attended. Crop tight, or use a different hook.
- **If it underperforms:** cut to one person's reaction (`reaction-face`) inside the crowd.

### 15. id: `story-countdown` · name: Countdown Sticker Opener
- **Formats:** story
- **Template:** Frame 1: "[Event] in [n] days" + countdown sticker
- **NUMA examples:** "Numa Nights · Friday · tap to get reminded" · "Clinical Conversations · next Tuesday · bring your questions"
- **Visual frame 0:** Best event photo full-bleed, sticker centered, 5 words max.
- **Best use:** event
- **Evidence:** measured. Frame 1 has the highest Story exit rate (23.8%, Socialinsider). Opinion on the sticker.
- **Expected effect:** frame-1 exit ↓ · completion ↑ · sends → · saves n/a
- **When NOT to use:** more than one countdown at a time.
- **If it underperforms:** open on a video clip from the last event, then put the sticker on frame 2.

### 16. id: `clinical-conversations-question` · name: Expert Asks the Question
- **Formats:** reel, carousel, story
- **Template:** "[Expert name/role]: '[open question]?' — [event, date]"
- **NUMA examples:** "'What should a first-timer know about the sauna?' Ask it live at Clinical Conversations." · "A local practitioner, an open mic, your questions. Tuesday."
- **Visual frame 0:** Expert's face, mid-sentence, with the question on screen.
- **Best use:** event, education
- **Evidence:** opinion. FTC: expert endorsements are treated as the advertiser's own claims, so clip only questions, framing and logistics, never efficacy statements.
- **Expected effect:** skip → · avg watch time ↑ · sends → · saves ↑
- **When NOT to use:** if the clip contains a health claim (even a hedged one). Cut it.
- **If it underperforms:** switch to an audience-question carousel ("What would you ask?").

### 17. id: `send-this-to` · name: Built for the DM
- **Formats:** reel, carousel
- **Template:** "Send this to the friend who [relatable trait]"
- **NUMA examples:** "Send this to the friend who says they 'run cold.'" · "Send this to whoever owes you a Thursday night plan."
- **Visual frame 0:** Two people reacting together, line on screen.
- **Best use:** community, collab
- **Evidence:** measured mechanism. Sends per reach is the key signal for unconnected reach (Mosseri, January 2025). Reels hold the highest share rate (0.10%, Socialinsider). The pattern itself: opinion.
- **Expected effect:** skip → · avg watch time → · sends ↑↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** every post. Explicit share-bait wears out quickly.
- **If it underperforms:** make the content itself sendable (`bring-a-friend-duo`) without saying "send."

### 18. id: `bring-a-friend-duo` · name: Two Friends, One Plunge
- **Formats:** reel, photo
- **Template:** "[Relationship] at [place]: [funny/sweet line]"
- **NUMA examples:** "Date night that doesn't involve a bar tab." · "Best friends, two plunges, one bad decision (it was great)."
- **Visual frame 0:** Two people at the plunge edge, mid-countdown or mid-laugh.
- **Best use:** community, offer
- **Evidence:** observed. SweatHouz couples UGC ("This is how we do couples therapy around here"). Othership's social positioning.
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends ↑↑ · saves →
- **When NOT to use:** if it makes solo visitors feel excluded. Alternate with solo stories.
- **If it underperforms:** let one person narrate the other's reaction (`reaction-face`).

### 19. id: `instructor-mid-sentence` · name: Already Talking
- **Formats:** reel
- **Template:** Start speaking mid-phrase: "…and that's why we [do X] at [place]." Text says the setup.
- **NUMA examples:** "…so round one is always the shortest." · "…and the breathwork starts before you ever touch cold water."
- **Visual frame 0:** Instructor at lens, mouth moving, studio behind, text overlay (4–7 words) that sets up rather than duplicates the line.
- **Best use:** education
- **Evidence:** opinion (practitioners: appear already looking at the lens, mid-sentence; text should set up the line, not repeat it).
- **Expected effect:** skip ↓ · avg watch time ↑ · sends → · saves ↑
- **When NOT to use:** if the instructor is uncomfortable on camera. Stiffness raises skips.
- **If it underperforms:** go voiceover over B-roll (`steam-pour` + narration).

### 20. id: `ritual-steps` · name: The NUMA Ritual in Steps
- **Formats:** carousel, reel
- **Template:** "Our [n]-round [ritual]: [step 1] → [step 2] → [step 3]"
- **NUMA examples:** "Our 3-round contrast flow, start to finish." · "Breathe. Heat. Cold. Repeat. Here's the order we teach."
- **Visual frame 0:** Numbered "1" over the first step's best image. Reel version: fast montage with step counters.
- **Best use:** education
- **Evidence:** measured format effect. Carousels earn 9× the saves of single images (Metricool 2026). The pattern: opinion.
- **Expected effect:** skip → · avg watch time ↑ · sends → · saves ↑↑

## Recommendations: The 10 to Test First

Run each as a pair: same body, different first 3 seconds. Reels go out as Trial Reels; per Instagram's Help Center as checked in Aug–Oct 2026, professional accounts qualify at 200 followers (the older 1,000 rule dates from July 2025), so NUMA's Business account is eligible. Judge on skip rate, average watch time and shares divided by reach against NUMA's own trailing median, not industry averages. Carousels are judged on saves per reach.

1. **`plunge-entry`**: highest observed ceiling in the category (Remedy Place at 2.7M–2.8M views) and fully claim-free.
2. **`steam-pour`**: the Finnish sauna is NUMA's 14-person differentiator, and Othership's sensory explainer ran about 10× its norm.
3. **`reaction-face`**: first-timer reactions are the dominant UGC hook at SweatHouz and Bathhouse. It also recruits members to become creators.
4. **`timer-challenge`**: turns the plunge into a shareable achievement, à la #6minuteclub, and pairs with the 30-day $49 offer as a "Day 1 vs Day 30" series (framed as comfort and fun, not health).
5. **`local-callout`**: competitors nearby are tiny, so "Mobile has a Finnish sauna now" is a novelty claim that's true, local and highly sendable.
6. **`price-anchor`**: tests whether the $49 offer converts directly. Run it to followers and a local audience, not as a cold Trial Reel.
7. **`event-crowd`**: Numa Nights footage sells a "night out" alternative, the positioning Othership's CEO calls the future of the category.
8. **`send-this-to` / `bring-a-friend-duo`** (test as one slot): sends are the signal Mosseri ties most to non-follower reach.
9. **`first-timer-faq` + `slide2-rehook`**: carousels win saves (9× images, Metricool), and the slide-2 re-serve doubles the hook's chances. It also removes first-visit anxiety, the main barrier for a new category in Mobile.
10. **`breathe-along`**: a uniquely NUMA asset (breathwork classes) that delivers value on the spot, should lift watch time and saves, and needs no claims.

**Cadence suggestion:** 3 Reels, 2 carousels and daily Stories per week. Rotate one new hook pair per week for 10 weeks. Retire any pattern whose skip rate sits above your median twice in a row, and use its "try instead" swap.

## Caveats

- **No measured study isolates hook types.** Socialinsider, Buffer and Metricool measure formats and engagement, not "plunge vs. steam" openings. Every per-pattern effect above is a directional expectation built on measured mechanics plus observed examples.
- **The best observed numbers are TikTok, not Instagram,** and they come from celebrity-adjacent brands (Remedy Place). Expect smaller absolute numbers at under 5,000 followers. Socialinsider's 1–5K tier averages about 3 comments per Reel.
- **Brand accounts underperform guest voices** in the observed data (Bathhouse brand TikToks at 85–304 likes vs a creator video at about 35.5K). Build member-generated content with written media releases, especially in swimwear areas.
- **Mute-viewing percentages** cited online trace to a May 2016 Digiday report about Facebook, not Instagram, based on publisher self-reporting (LittleThings and Mic at about 85% soundless views, PopSugar at 50–80%). They're directionally consistent but not verified Instagram data.
- **FTC risk sits in captions, voiceovers and testimonials as much as in hooks.** SweatHouz-style "reduce soreness by 40%" lines, or a member saying "it cured my back," would put NUMA on the hook for substantiation it can't provide.
