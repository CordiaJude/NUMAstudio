# Numa Studio: Katie's Guide

Version 2.1, updated 2026-11-15. The live, shareable copy is the Claude Doc "NUMA Studio: Katie's Guide"; this file is the same text for the Claude Project's knowledge.

Numa Studio plans, builds and measures Numa's Instagram on its own; you film what each day's card asks for and approve each post before it goes out. This guide covers every part of version 2.1, day to day.

## Your whole job

You do three things. Claude does everything else: planning, editing, captions, music, checks, posting and learning what works.

1. **Film what the card asks for.** Each day's Drive folder has a SHOOT CARD. Film or photograph what it lists and drop the files in that folder. Most cards take 5 to 15 minutes. Some say "NO FILMING NEEDED".
2. **Approve each post.** When a post is ready, its row in the plan Sheet says "Ready to post". Look at it, then change the row to Approved. Nothing goes out until a person does this.
3. **Steer when you want to (optional).** Leave a note in a day folder, edit a row in the plan Sheet, or ask Claude in the Numa Studio Project. Claude never waits for you and never asks you questions.

That's it. If you skip filming one day, Claude works around it. If you don't approve a post, it simply waits.

## A normal day

On a normal day you spend about 15 minutes: one look and one tap to approve, plus filming when a card asks. All times are Central.

| Time | What Claude does | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5:00 am | Builds today's post from today's folder: edits it, writes the caption, checks it, saves it to the folder's `Finished/` and marks the Sheet row "Ready to post". Sends you one short note. | Nothing. |
| Morning | — | Open the note or the Sheet. Look at the post in `Finished/`. If it's good, set its row to Approved (see Approving posts). |
| Noon onward | Posts approved posts at their planned time. The earliest posting time is 12:00. Also pulls Story numbers at 12:40. | Nothing. |
| Any time during the day | — | Film tomorrow's card (Mon to Thu, 9 to 11 am, is the best batch time) and drop the files in tomorrow's folder. |
| 8:00 pm | Checks tomorrow's folder against its card, finds anything missing, and posts any approved post still due. Sends you a heads-up, never a question. | Read the heads-up if you like. |
| 10:00 pm | Pulls the latest Instagram numbers, updates the dashboard and what it has learned. | Nothing. |
| Every hour your Mac is awake | Does the few steps that need Chrome on your Mac: Trial Reels, collab invites, the AI label. | Keep the Mac on when you can, with Claude open. It's fine if it sleeps; Claude catches up. |

If you approve after 9 pm, the post goes out the next day from noon.

## Your week and your month

Once a week you read the Monday digest; once a month a new plan appears on its own. Nobody approves the plan.

| When | What happens | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fridays, 7 pm | Claude looks for three trend ideas that fit Numa (formats and hooks, never songs) and checks the weather for the week. It may add one as a Trial Reel. | Nothing. |
| Sundays, 6 pm | The **Monday digest** arrives: last week's results, what Claude learned, posts still waiting for approval, this week's schedule, and the shoot list grouped by room. It's a Google Doc in `_Plan/Digests/`. | Read it Monday morning, about 5 minutes. Approve anything listed as waiting. |
| The 24th, 9 pm | Claude plans next month: the plan Sheet rows, a Drive folder per day, and a SHOOT CARD in each. | Skim next month's rows if you like. Edit any row you want changed. |
| Every day, 10 pm | The **dashboard** updates: every post's numbers against Numa's own normal, plus "Do more of" and "Do less of". | Open it whenever you're curious. Jude has the link. |

**How Claude learns.** It compares every post with Numa's own usual numbers, never with other accounts. It gives each post a first read after 3 days and a final score after 7. Most posts use what's working; about 1 in 3 tries something new, one change at a time. It learns which hooks, post types and how many posts a week work best. It's slow on purpose: a clear winner shows after about 3 to 4 months. The "What we learned" part of the digest tells you what it changed and why.

## Approving posts

A post goes out only after a person sets its row in the plan Sheet to Approved, after it is built. It takes about a minute on your phone.

**To approve:**

1. Open the **Numa 30-Day Reel Plan** Sheet (Google Sheets app, or Drive → Numa Reels → _Plan).
2. Find rows whose Status says **Ready to post**. Tap the row's **Folder link**.
3. In that day folder, open `Finished/`. Watch the reel, or swipe through the carousel or photo. Open the caption file (it ends in `_copy.txt`) and read the caption Claude chose.
4. Back in the Sheet, change Status to **Approved**, or type **Approved - KM** with your initials so the record shows who approved it.
5. Done. Claude posts it at its planned time, or at the next open slot if that time has passed. The row then says **Posted** with the Instagram link.

**Good to know:**

- **Approve after it's built.** Setting Approved on a row that still says Planned or Shot doesn't count; Claude sets it back to Ready to post so you can look first.
- **Don't like it?** Leave the row as Ready to post and tell Claude what to change, in the Project ("Redo Day 07 with fewer fast cuts") or in a `note.txt` in the day folder. It rebuilds and the row comes back to Ready to post. Don't want it at all? Tell Claude to drop it.
- **Posts that show a member** also need that member's yes on record (see Members and consent). Without it the post waits, even if you approved it.
- **If a carousel's words are edited after you approve it**, it comes back to Ready to post so you can look again. A change to how it looks, with the same words, posts as approved.
- **Trial Reels, collab posts and posts that need Instagram's AI label** go out from your Mac through Chrome. If the Mac is off at posting time, they move to the next day. After two misses they come back for you to approve again.
- **One notice per batch.** You get one short message when posts are waiting, never one per post.
- **Who approves?** Until the owners decide, either of you or the social media lead can approve.

## Reading a shoot card

Every day folder has a SHOOT CARD that tells you exactly what to capture, by when, and where to put it. Here is one:

```
DAY 09 · Wed Dec 9 · "Your first plunge"    FILM BY: Mon Dec 7 (batch shoot: plunge room)

WHERE TO PUT FILES
  Drive > Numa Reels > 2026-12 December > Day 09 - 2026-12-09 - Your first plunge
  Photos -> photos/  Videos -> clips/
  Voice memo -> "voiceover 09"; re-record by replacing it. Music optional.
  Any file names. Sheet edits optional.

WHAT TO FILM  (vertical, 4K 30 fps, hold 5 s, no zoom)
  [ ] 1. Close-up: feet stepping into the plunge, water rippling
  [ ] 2. Wide: the plunge room from the doorway, steam from the sauna behind
  [ ] 3. Hands on the edge of the plunge, slow breath
  Also snap: the thermometer at the plunge

READ THIS OUT LOUD (Scott reads this)  (closet or carpeted room, phone 12 in away, normal voice, one take, 1 s silence each end)
  "Your first plunge is shorter than you think ..."

MUSIC: calm, 90 BPM  LENGTH: 24 s (3 shots + library).
IF YOU CAN'T: skip it. Claude will find another way.
```

**What each part means:**

- **FILM BY** is the day you should have it filmed, usually a batch day earlier in the week, grouped by room.
- **WHAT TO FILM** is a short checklist, usually 3 to 5 shots. The phone settings are in brackets.
- **Also snap** lists still photos Claude would like.
- **READ THIS OUT LOUD** is the voiceover. Scott records it (see Voiceovers).
- **IF YOU CAN'T** means exactly that: skip it and Claude uses the media library, brand photos or a different topic.

**Other kinds of cards:**

| The card says | What it is | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A shot list and a script | A reel | Film the shots; Scott records the script. |
| NO FILMING NEEDED. Claude designs this carousel in Claude Design | A designed carousel (text slides with Numa's photos) | Nothing. You can edit it before it posts (see What you get back). |
| NO FILMING NEEDED. Claude builds this photo carousel from the day's photos | A photo carousel | Drop any good photos from that day into `photos/` if you have them. |
| NO FILMING NEEDED. Claude builds this photo post | A single photo post | Same: photos welcome, not required. |
| NO FILMING NEEDED. Claude cuts today's Stories | Stories | Nothing. |
| POST: Trial Reel | A reel shown only to non-followers first, as a test | Same as a reel. |

## Filming and photos

Use the Numa iPhone 14 Pro, hold it upright, and keep each shot steady for 5 seconds. Anyone working in the studio can film.

**How to film:**

- **Vertical**, 4K at 30 fps (Settings → Camera → Record Video).
- **Hold each shot 5 seconds** without moving. Claude picks the best moment.
- **No zoom.** Walk closer instead.
- **Light:** face the window or turn the lights up. Dark clips get brightened, but bright is better.
- **Sound:** natural sound is welcome (the splash, the stones). Don't talk over shots unless the card asks.
- **Photos:** hold the phone upright too. Real people and the real room beat empty rooms; graphics, quote cards and posters are not wanted.
- **Best batch time:** Monday to Thursday, 9 to 11 am. On Tuesday there's a yoga class, so film in the Flow Space.

**Who may be on camera:**

| Who | OK? |
| --- | --- |
| Scott and Katie | Yes |
| Instructors and staff | Yes |
| Members | Only with their yes on record in the Consent List (see Members and consent) |
| Children | **Never**, for now. The owners haven't decided yet. |
| Anyone else, or someone you're not sure about | No. Point the camera away, or leave them out of frame. |

**Never film:**

- Counseling rooms while a client is in them, or without the counselor's permission
- Bathrooms and locker rooms while people are changing
- The kitchen and breakroom

If someone without a yes ends up in a clip by accident, still drop it in. Claude blurs or crops them, or doesn't use the shot.

## Voiceovers

Scott records every voiceover, and there are no cloned or copied voices. A voiceover takes him about 2 minutes on his phone.

1. Open the day's SHOOT CARD and find **READ THIS OUT LOUD**.
2. Go somewhere quiet and soft: a closet or a carpeted room. Hold the phone about 12 inches away.
3. Open Voice Memos, tap record, wait a second, read the lines in a normal voice, wait a second, stop. One take is fine; small stumbles are fixed.
4. Rename the memo **voiceover 09** (the day's number) and put it in that day's folder. To re-record, replace the file.

Scott can record several days in one sitting; Claude matches each memo to its day. If there's no recording on build day, the reel uses music and on-screen text instead. Nothing waits on him.

Everything lives in the Google Drive folder **Numa Reels**: one folder per month, one per day, and `_Plan` for the shared files.

```

      SHOOT CARD          what to capture
      photos/             your photos go here
      clips/              your videos go here
      voiceover 09        Scott's voice memo
      note.txt            optional: your note to Claude
      Finished/           Claude puts the finished post here
  _Plan/
    Numa 30-Day Reel Plan    the plan Sheet (where you approve)
    Consent List             who has said yes to being shown
    Closures                 days Numa is closed
    Digests/                 the Monday digests
    Music Library/           music Claude may use
```

**From your phone:** open the Google Drive app, go to the day folder, tap **+**, then **Upload**, pick the photos and videos. Put videos in `clips/` and photos in `photos/` if you can; if they land in the day folder itself, Claude sorts them.

**From the Mac:** drag the files into the day folder in a browser or Finder.

**File names don't matter**, and iPhone photos (HEIC) are fine. Uploading into the wrong day? Move them to the right folder before 8 pm the night before; the night check looks then.

## What you get back

Each build fills the day's `Finished/` folder and sends you one short note: what it made, the one-line story, the caption it chose, and the choices that matter.

| File ends in | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `_reel.mp4` | The finished reel, ready for Instagram |
| `_reach.mp4` | A shorter cut, for Trial Reels and reaching new people |
| `_hook_1.mp4`, `_hook_2.mp4` | The same reel with a different opening, for tests |
| `_cover.jpg` | The reel's cover image |
| `_carousel_01.jpg`, `_02` … | Carousel slides, in order |
| `_story_1.jpg` … | Story frames |
| `_copy.txt` | The chosen caption, the other two options, 3 to 5 hashtags, a Story line and a short email paragraph |
| `.srt` | Captions file for the video |

**Editing a designed carousel.** Designed carousels are made in Claude Design, and the note includes a link to the canvas.

1. Open the link and click into any slide to change the words.
2. Keep it to Numa's facts and words, no emojis, and leave no `[brackets]` behind; a slide with a leftover placeholder won't post.
3. Any web link on a slide must be Numa's own (linktr.ee/numawc), or the slide won't post.
4. Claude re-renders the slides right before posting, so your edit is what goes out. If you change the words after approving, the post comes back to Ready to post for one more look.

Want a different caption? Pick one of the other two in `_copy.txt` and tell Claude in the Project, or put it in `note.txt` before you approve.

## Steering

You can change anything at any time in three ways; Claude reads them every run and never waits for them.

**1. A note in the day folder.** Create a file called `note.txt` in the day folder and write what you want, in plain words. Claude reads it at the next build.

- "Push the $49 Discover Numa this week."
- "Use the photo of the steam, not the empty room."
- "Fewer fast cuts."

A note like "fewer fast cuts" is remembered for every future post, so you only say it once.

**2. The plan Sheet.** Edit any row: the title, the hook, the script, the call to action, the date. Claude re-reads the row every run. To move a post, change its date. To drop one, ask Claude.

**3. Ask Claude in the Numa Studio Project.** Open the Claude app, open the **Numa Studio** Project, and type what you want:

| You type | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "Make a reel for Date Night on Jan 15." | It plans one, writes a shoot card, and adds it to the plan |
| "Redo Day 07 with fewer fast cuts." | It rebuilds that post; the row goes back to Ready to post |
| "How did last week's posts do?" | It answers from the dashboard numbers |
| "What should I film this week?" | It lists this week's shots grouped by room |
| "Drop Day 12." | It takes that post off the plan |
| "We're closed Friday." | It adds the closure and moves that day's post |

Keep requests to things Numa really offers. Claude won't invent offers, discount codes or events; tell it the details and it uses them.

## Members and consent

A member appears in a post only if their yes is written down first. Claude checks this before every post, even after you approve.

**Getting a yes:**

1. Ask the member yourself, one person at a time, before filming them or using their words.
2. Write it down in the Consent List (Drive → Numa Reels → _Plan → Consent List): their name, the date, and what they said yes to (photo, video, their words, first name shown).
3. Ask separately if their face may be used in AI-animated clips. Until you record a yes for that, Claude never sends a member's face to AI.

**If someone changes their mind:** mark it in the Consent List. Claude stops any post with them that hasn't gone out yet and lists any that already did, so you can take those down.

**What members say in posts.** Their words describe the experience ("it's the one hour my phone stays in the locker"), never a health result ("my back pain is gone"). If a member got a free session for taking part, the caption says so; Claude adds it when you tell it.

**Children never appear** in posts for now, even with a parent's yes, until the owners decide otherwise.

## The rules Claude always follows

These come from your setup answers and are checked automatically on every caption, slide and voiceover line; a post that breaks one doesn't go out.

- **The name** is always "Numa" or "Numa Wellness Collective", never "NUMA". The trial is "Discover Numa"; the free track is "The Numa Reset".
- **Sell the experience, never a health result.** Nothing treats, heals, cures or prevents anything; no promised results. Red light "supports" or "improves", never "restores". No claims that the sauna clears alcohol or the plunge lowers inflammation. Never "HSA/FSA eligible". No comparisons to other studios.
- **Facts stay true.** Plunges 44 to 52°F, sauna 175 to 190°F, real hours, real prices. The sauna's size and type aren't stated until they're confirmed. Clinical Conversations isn't promoted while it's cancelled.
- **Offers are only yours.** Discover Numa ($49, one month, 3 classes, 3 red light sessions, unlimited Flow Space, ends on its own) and the offers in your answers. NEWBIE never appears in public posts. New people are never sent to Saturday morning classes.
- **Your words.** It uses "take a deep breath", "calm in the chaos", "show up", "return to yourself", "Visit Passes". Never "cheap", "credits", "Discover Pass". No emojis. No quote cards, flyers or posters.
- **Music** is licensed or the real sound of the room, never a trending song.
- **AI is labeled.** Any post with AI-animated people or AI-made scenes gets Instagram's AI label.
- **The "57 minutes sauna, 11 minutes cold" line** stays out of posts until its source is recorded.

## When something's wrong

Claude fixes almost everything on its own; anything only a person can fix goes to Jude, not to you.

| What you notice | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A post isn't up, and its row says Ready to post | It hasn't been approved yet | Look at it and set the row to Approved |
| You approved it, but it's not up yet | It waits for its planned time, or the next slot (never before noon or after 9 pm) | Nothing; it goes out on its own |
| Row says Approved, but it came back to Ready to post | It was approved before it was built, or a carousel's words changed after approval | Look again and re-approve |
| A member post won't go out | That member's yes isn't in the Consent List | Add their yes, or ask Claude to swap the shot |
| "Ready to post (Trial Reel)" or "(collab)" or "(AI label)" stays for a day | It needs Chrome on your Mac, and the Mac was off | Leave the Mac on with Claude open; it goes the next day. Or post it from the Instagram app using the file in Finished/ |
| The post looks wrong (wrong photo, bad cut, wrong caption) | — | Don't approve it. Tell Claude what to change in the Project or in note.txt |
| A post went up twice | Shouldn't happen; Claude checks before every post | Delete the extra one in Instagram and tell Jude |
| No post was built for a day | Missing material, or the build failed and Claude rescheduled | Check the 8 pm heads-up or the digest; it says what happened |
| The day's folder is empty and you can't film | — | Nothing. Claude uses the library or swaps the topic |
| Instagram, Drive or another connection stops working | A login expired | Tell Jude; he reconnects it |

You'll never be asked a question by the system during a run. If something needs a person, Jude gets one line with the fix.
